1. EDKnrc energy deposition kernels (conehead)
==============================================================================

Monoenergetic point energy deposition kernels in water (0.5-6.0 MeV),
generated with EGSnrc/EDKnrc and published in the kernels/ directory of the
conehead repository by Samuel Peet: https://github.com/samuelpeet/conehead

Modification: the .egslst tables ("total" column) were read and stored as
energy fraction per (cone, sphere) bin in
data/kernels/edknrc_mono/edk_water_mono.npz.

2. NIST XCOM photon cross sections
==============================================================================

The mass attenuation coefficients of liquid water (28 energies between 0.01
and 20 MeV, total attenuation with coherent scattering) used in
calcindep/physics.py come from:

  M.J. Berger, J.H. Hubbell, S.M. Seltzer, J. Chang, J.S. Coursey, R. Sukumar,
  D.S. Zucker and K. Olsen. XCOM: Photon Cross Sections Database, NIST
  Standard Reference Database 8 (XGAM). National Institute of Standards and
  Technology, Gaithersburg, MD. https://dx.doi.org/10.18434/T48G6X

(c) 1990, 1998 copyright by the U.S. Secretary of Commerce on behalf of the
United States of America. All rights reserved.

NIST provides these data as a public service and assumes no liability for
their use. Permission to use NIST data is subject to providing appropriate
acknowledgment of NIST's creation of the data, which is given here.

4. Published methods (no third-party code)
==============================================================================

The following code is original, but it implements published methods:

  - Collapsed cone superposition: A. Ahnesjo, "Collapsed cone convolution of
    radiant energy for photon dose calculation in heterogeneous media", Med.
    Phys. 16(4), 577-592 (1989).
  - Energy deposition kernels: T.R. Mackie, A.F. Bielajew, D.W.O. Rogers and
    J.J. Battista, "Generation of photon energy deposition kernels using the
    EGS Monte Carlo code", Phys. Med. Biol. 33, 1-20 (1988).
  - Gamma index: D.A. Low, W.B. Harms, S. Mutic and J.A. Purdy, "A technique
    for the quantitative evaluation of dose distributions", Med. Phys. 25(5),
    656-661 (1998).
  - Non-negative least squares: C.L. Lawson and R.J. Hanson, Solving Least
    Squares Problems, Prentice-Hall (1974).
